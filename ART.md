# Art provenance

Three original panels generated specifically for «Последний сигнал», IseFDK, with OpenAI built-in image generation on 2026-10-05. All final pixels were inspected. No imagery was copied from the reference website.

Palette: midnight ink, ice blue, burnt vermilion and pale paper. Editorial risograph/woodcut with gouache grain.

## Optimization

Original PNG files copied non-destructively. WebP variants use Pillow Lanczos resampling only, no creative pixel editing; 1600px width quality 88 and 960px width quality 85, method 6.
